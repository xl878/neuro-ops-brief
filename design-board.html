For approval timeouts, the Accountable role is the next tier in the leadership chain. The brief names the Clinical Lead as approver, and Sevaro later said Ops Leads make real-time decisions, so the approver is a sidebar setting in the app. The off-shift doctor isn't in the RACI because they only accept or decline. Two routing protections no one asked for

Both come from looking past the least-busy doctor.

1Innovation3Impact

Don't swamp the new doctor

Routing favours whoever has the fewest consults. A doctor who has just joined has zero, so we start her at the team median instead.

Default routing
10 of 20 calls
Our plan

Spend the scarce doctor last

When reassigning, both candidates pass every hard rule. We then count how many other at-risk hospitals would lose their last eligible backup.

Controlled test case CON-2200. The least busy doctor, Dr. Patel, would cost two hospitals their last backup. Dr. Ramirez costs one, so she gets the consult.

A benchmark result we kept, even though it doesn't flatter us

Mean queue wait, capped at 120 min, over the same 6,641 replayed consults. A "specialized" care policy made waits much worse. ML ranking barely moved them. So rules stay in charge, and the models only advise.
